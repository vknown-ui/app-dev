# My Favorite Movie Franchise: John Wick

**John Wick** is a neo-noir action thriller franchise starring *Keanu Reeves* as a legendary retired hitman who is forced back into the criminal underworld he thought he left behind.

> *"People keep asking if I'm back and I haven't really had an answer. Now yeah, I'm thinkin' I'm back!"* — John Wick

## Why It Stands Out
* **Choreography & Action:** Features realistic *"Gun-Fu"* combat with long, unbroken camera takes instead of shaky-cam cuts.
* **World-Building:** Introduces an intricate secret society of assassins governed by strict rules, gold coins, and neutral safe havens like **The Continental Hotel**.
* **Cinematography:** Uses vibrant neon lighting and sleek visual storytelling.
